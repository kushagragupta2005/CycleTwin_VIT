# Assumptions

Every physiological value and threshold below is a literature-informed assumption (verify with clinician). Values configure a synthetic engineering exercise and are not clinical parameters.

- The cohort contains nine phenotype archetypes at equal generator frequency (one ninth each). This is a simulation mixture, not a prevalence estimate.
- EHR-lite exposes each simulated patient's archetype, insulin-resistance latent and androgen load, plus synthetic age (18–42), BMI and family-history draw. The latent and androgen load influence the label-generating process. Therefore, tier 3 results are **oracle-adjacent, simulator-internal checks**, not real EHR-fusion results.
- Insulin-resistance latent is sampled from a standard normal distribution and clipped to [−2.5, 2.5]. Androgen load is sampled with standard deviation 0.7 units and mean 0.65 in simulated PMOS archetypes or 0 in other archetypes. Stress is standard normal.
- Simulated ovulation day has nominal means 16, 22 or 26 days by archetype, base standard deviation 4 days, an insulin-resistance adjustment of 6 days per positive latent unit, and a stress adjustment of 3 days per standardised unit for selected phenotypes.
- Selected archetypes receive an anovulation probability of 0.24 + 0.09 × positive insulin-resistance latent. Non-anovulatory cycle length is ovulation day + 13 days with standard deviation 1.8 days; anovulatory length is drawn from 24–41 days.
- E1 labels a synthetic cycle positive if ovulation is absent or occurs after cycle day 21. Forecast snapshots are cycle days 10, 14 and 18. These are simulator target settings, not clinical thresholds.
- Menstrual log error is sampled from −2, 0, 0, 0, +1 or +3 days. Temperature offset noise has standard deviation 0.035 °C, a post-ovulation rise of 0.20 °C, random-walk increments with standard deviation 0.004 °C, and 12% missing nights.
- Resting-heart-rate offset noise has standard deviation 1 synthetic unit and a post-ovulation offset of +0.35 units; HRV offset noise has standard deviation 1 and a post-ovulation offset of −0.25 units. Steps use a log-normal distribution with parameters 8.5 and 0.25; sleep uses a 7-hour mean and 0.75-hour standard deviation. These are not calibrated consumer-device distributions.
- Tier 1 risk uses a coefficient of 0.20 on the mean resting-heart-rate offset and a sigmoid intercept of −0.15. Tier 2 adds a −5.0 coefficient on observed temperature change and a −0.9 intercept. The coverline uses a 0.12 °C rise; the ovulation estimate fallback is forecast day + 10.
- The illustrative twin uses follicle growth 0.065 units/day, androgen and insulin-resistance deductions 0.025 and 0.012 units/day, growth noise standard deviation 0.003, surge threshold 1.0, progesterone-like state relaxation of 0.5/day, and a two-day temperature lag. Temperature rise is 0.20 °C with 0.02 °C observation noise; resting-HR offset is 1.1 bpm with 0.6 bpm noise.
- The metabolic twin uses 15-minute Euler steps, 95 mg/dL baseline glucose, insulin baseline 10 units, 0.020/hour glucose clearance, 0.10/hour insulin decay, 0.40/hour meal-compartment decay, 0.04 meal-to-insulin gain, 0.05 insulin-state decay, 0.0015 insulin-to-sensitivity gain, a 0.05 luteal insulin-sensitivity reduction, meal amplitude 28 mg/dL with log-scale noise 0.25, Gaussian 5 mg/dL observation noise, and a 40–300 mg/dL numerical clamp. These values are literature-informed assumptions (verify with clinician). The code is illustrative and is not a clinical hormone or glucose model.
- Dashboard safety flags map simulated thyroid-mimic, hormonal-contraception and early-menarche archetypes to abstention. Wear completeness below 0.75 triggers abstention; the age gate is under 18. These are demo settings, not clinical policy.
- The alert cutoff is 0.50 with at most one alert per patient-cycle. Split-conformal calibration uses alpha 0.10. These are illustrative settings, not clinical thresholds; coverage is not reported in the G1 benchmark.
- Synthetic CGM uses a baseline 98 mg/dL plus 5 mg/dL per positive insulin-resistance unit, three meal pulses per day at nominal hours 8, 13 and 19, meal-size log-normal parameters 0 and 0.35, pulse amplitude (24 + 10 × positive insulin-resistance latent), exponential decay scale 4 samples over a maximum 12 15-minute samples, 7 mg/dL observation noise, and a +3 mg/dL luteal-phase offset beginning day 16. This is a software fixture, not a glucose physiology model.
- The CGM excursion flag is >140 mg/dL within 120 minutes after a synthetic meal. This is a literature-informed assumption (verify with clinician), not a clinical decision threshold.
- Dashboard what-if ranges use hand-set risk multipliers of 0.80–0.98 for sleep, 0.75–0.98 for walking, 0.80–1.00 for food swap, 0.80–1.00 for stress, and 0.75–0.98 for weight change. These are illustrative only and are not treatment recommendations.

The labels, covariates, intervention multipliers and sensor noise are synthetic. No physiological model is clinically validated.
